Research a topic.

1. Ask the topic — be specific (e.g. "FMCSA ELD short-haul exemption 2026 update," not "ELDs").
2. Pick the right subfolder under `05-research/`:
   - DOT/FMCSA/safety reg → `DOT-safety/`
   - Business operations → `business-systems/`
   - AI tools → `AI-tools/`
   - Competitor → `competitors/`
   - Specific regulation reference → `regulations/`
   - General reference → `references/`
3. Use `07-templates/Research-Note.md`.
4. File output: `05-research/<subfolder>/<kebab-case-topic>.md`.
5. Note structure:
   - **Question**: what I asked.
   - **Findings**: bulleted, sourced. No claim without a source.
   - **Sources**: full URLs or citations. For regulations, FMCSR section number.
   - **Implications for my work**: 2-4 bullets — what changes for Select Safety, programs, or clients.
   - **Follow-ups**: action items, linked to projects/programs they affect.
6. Add `[[wiki-links]]` to related procedures, programs, or earlier research.
7. If the topic touches an active client or program, ping me to update that note too.

When you don't know something current, say so — don't invent.
