Review my active programs.

1. Scan `02-programs-systems/active-programs/` and `02-programs-systems/builds/`.
2. For each, report:
   - Name + one-line description
   - Current `build_stage`
   - `next_action`
   - Days since `updated`
   - Blockers (anything with `status: blocked` or noted in the file)
3. Categorize:
   - **Moving** — updated this week, has a clear next action
   - **Stale** — no update in 14+ days, still marked active
   - **Stuck** — has a blocker noted
   - **Ready to advance** — current stage's checklist is complete
4. End with a recommended action per program: continue, unblock how, archive, or move to next stage.
5. Don't make changes. Just give me the report.
