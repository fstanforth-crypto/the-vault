# DOT: Driver Qualification File

**Regulation / FMCSR section:** 49 CFR 391, esp. 391.51
**Applies to:** All drivers operating CMVs subject to Part 391.
**Effective date:** As of hire.
**Next review:** Annual.

## Summary

A Driver Qualification (DQ) file must be maintained by the motor carrier for each driver. Contents are mandated by 49 CFR 391.51. Records must be kept for the duration of employment and for 3 years after termination (with exceptions noted below).

## Requirements — DQ file contents

- [ ] **Application for employment** — 49 CFR 391.21. Retain for duration of employment + 3 years.
- [ ] **Inquiry to previous employers** — 49 CFR 391.23(a)(2). Retain 3 years from date of inquiry.
- [ ] **Inquiry to State agencies (3-year driving record)** — 49 CFR 391.23(a)(1). At hire and annually thereafter.
- [ ] **Annual review of driving record** — 49 CFR 391.25. Retain for duration of employment + 3 years.
- [ ] **Annual driver's list of violations / certificate of violations** — 49 CFR 391.27 (if still required by carrier; FMCSA largely phased out for CDL drivers via Clearinghouse / driver record check — verify current rule).
- [ ] **Road test certificate or equivalent** — 49 CFR 391.31 or 391.33. Retain for duration of employment + 3 years.
- [ ] **Medical Examiner's Certificate (long form not required since 2014; cert is sufficient)** — 49 CFR 391.41-391.45. Retain current cert + history.
- [ ] **Medical Examiner's National Registry verification** — confirm examiner on NRCME at time of exam.
- [ ] **For CDL holders: CDLIS / state Medical Certification Status** — many states post electronically; verify status.
- [ ] **Drug & alcohol pre-employment test results** — 49 CFR 382.301 / 382.413. Retain per Part 382.
- [ ] **FMCSA Clearinghouse query** — pre-employment full query and annual limited queries. Retain query records 3 years.
- [ ] **Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) certificate** (if hired after Feb 7, 2022 and required) — 49 CFR 380 Subpart F.
- [ ] **Disqualification records** (if applicable).

## Records retention

- Most items: duration of employment + 3 years (49 CFR 391.51(d)).
- Drug/alcohol: per Part 382 (varies; some records 5 years).
- Clearinghouse: 3 years from query.

## Verification / audit method

A DOT auditor will spot-check a sample of DQ files. Each file should be complete, indexed in a consistent order, and free of pre-employment gaps. Common audit findings:
- Missing or expired medical certificate
- Missing previous-employer inquiries
- Annual MVR not on file or beyond 12-month interval
- Missing Clearinghouse pre-employment / annual queries
- Application incomplete (employment history, accident history)
