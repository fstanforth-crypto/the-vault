# Safety Procedure: Pre-Trip Inspection (CMV)

**Purpose:** Identify defects or deficiencies that would affect the safe operation of a commercial motor vehicle before it enters service.
**Applies to:** All drivers operating CMVs subject to FMCSRs.
**Hazards addressed:** Brake failure, tire failure, lighting failure, steering failure, coupling failure, leaking fluids, cargo securement failure.
**PPE required:** Safety vest (when on shoulder/roadway), gloves (when handling chains/couplings), eye protection (when checking under hood).
**Effective date:** 2026-05-21

## Steps

1. **Documents & inside cab.** Verify CDL, medical certificate, valid registration and insurance card present. Check last DVIR (49 CFR 396.13) — review and sign-off on any noted defects and that repairs were certified.
2. **Approach & engine off.** Walk around looking for leaks under the vehicle, obvious damage, items hanging loose.
3. **Engine compartment.** Check oil, coolant, power steering, washer fluid levels. Inspect belts, hoses, wiring for cracks or chafing. Check for leaks.
4. **In-cab start.** Start engine. Check oil pressure, ammeter/voltmeter, coolant temp gauges within normal range within first 2 minutes.
5. **Brake check.** Test parking brake (drive in low gear, brake should hold). Test service brake (5 mph, firm pedal pressure — vehicle should stop straight, no pull, no air loss alarm).
6. **Air brake check (if applicable).** Three-step air brake test per 49 CFR 393.52:
   - Governor cut-out (~120-140 psi) and cut-in (~100 psi)
   - Air loss rate ≤ 2 psi/min single, ≤ 3 psi/min combo, engine off, brake released
   - Low air warning by ~60 psi; spring brakes engage by ~20-45 psi
7. **Lights & signals.** Headlights (high/low), turn signals, 4-way flashers, brake lights, marker lights, reflectors.
8. **Tires, wheels, rims.** Tread depth ≥ 4/32" steer, ≥ 2/32" other. No cuts, bulges, or exposed cord. Lug nuts present, no rust streaks. Valve stems intact.
9. **Coupling system (combination vehicles).** Fifth wheel locked, jaws closed around kingpin, no gap, release arm secure. Safety chains/glad hands properly connected.
10. **Cargo securement.** Per 49 CFR 393 Subpart I — appropriate tiedowns, no shifting, weight within axle ratings.
11. **Emergency equipment.** Per 49 CFR 393.95 — fire extinguisher charged, 3 warning triangles, spare fuses (if not breaker-protected).
12. **Document on DVIR.** Driver completes pre-trip inspection report; signs only if no defects, or notes all defects requiring attention.

## Emergency actions

- Any defect affecting safe operation → vehicle out of service until repaired and certified per 49 CFR 396.11(c).
- Critical defects (brakes, steering, tires, coupling): do not move the vehicle. Notify dispatch and maintenance.

## Training required

- New driver: hands-on demonstration before independent operation. See [[01-select-safety/training/]].
- Annual refresher.
- Documented per 49 CFR 391.

## Records to keep

- Completed DVIRs — retain 3 months minimum (49 CFR 396.11(c)(2)) or longer per company policy.
- Repair certifications when defects were noted.
- Training records — 3 years from date of training (49 CFR 391.51 retention rules apply for related driver records).
