# Recordkeeping Retention

> Minimum FMCSA / OSHA retention periods. Carrier policy may exceed these — match the longer of regulation, insurance, or contract.

## Driver records (49 CFR 391.51)

| Record | Minimum retention |
|---|---|
| Application for employment | Duration of employment + 3 years |
| Inquiries to previous employers | 3 years from inquiry |
| Annual MVR | 3 years |
| Annual review of driving record | Duration of employment + 3 years |
| Road test certificate | Duration of employment + 3 years |
| Medical examiner's certificate (current) | While in effect |
| Medical certificate (history) | Per carrier policy (recommend 3 years) |
| Disqualification records | 3 years |

## Drug & alcohol (49 CFR 382.401)

| Record | Retention |
|---|---|
| Negative test results | 1 year |
| Verified positive test results | 5 years |
| Random testing selections / pool | 5 years |
| Refusals, alcohol ≥ 0.02 | 5 years |
| Post-accident testing | 5 years |
| Reasonable suspicion testing | 5 years |
| Return-to-duty / follow-up | 5 years |
| Calibration of EBT equipment | 2 years |
| Driver training on D&A program | While employed + 2 years after |
| Supervisor training | While employed + 2 years after |
| Annual MIS reports | 5 years |

## Clearinghouse (49 CFR 382.701-725)

| Action | Retention |
|---|---|
| Pre-employment full query | 3 years |
| Annual limited query | 3 years |
| Driver consent records | 3 years |

## Hours of service (49 CFR 395)

| Record | Retention |
|---|---|
| Driver duty status records (RODS / ELD) | 6 months |
| Supporting documents | 6 months |
| ELD malfunction records | 6 months |
| Driver investigation history (prior 3 years) | While driver employed |

## Vehicle (49 CFR 396)

| Record | Retention |
|---|---|
| DVIRs noting defects | 3 months |
| Repair certifications | 3 months (with DVIR) |
| Annual periodic inspection (396.17) | 14 months |
| Maintenance / inspection records | 1 year while vehicle controlled + 6 months after |
| Brake inspector qualifications | While employed + 1 year |

## Hazmat (49 CFR 172.704, 177)

| Record | Retention |
|---|---|
| Hazmat training records | 3 years past completion |
| Shipping papers (carrier) | 1 year (3 years for hazardous waste) |

## Incidents / accidents (49 CFR 390.15)

| Record | Retention |
|---|---|
| Accident register | 3 years |
| Copies of accident reports | 3 years |
| Insurance claims, citations | Per carrier policy |

## OSHA (29 CFR 1904)

| Record | Retention |
|---|---|
| OSHA 300 log, 300A summary, 301 incident report | 5 years after year of record |
| Medical / exposure records (where applicable) | 30 years (29 CFR 1910.1020) |
