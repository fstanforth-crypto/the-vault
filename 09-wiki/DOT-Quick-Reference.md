# DOT Quick Reference

> Working reference. Always verify against current eCFR before citing in a client deliverable: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-III

## Hours of Service (49 CFR 395) — property-carrying CMV

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| Driving time | 11 hours after 10 consecutive hours off |
| Driving window | 14 consecutive hours |
| 30-minute break | After 8 cumulative hours of driving |
| Weekly limit | 60 hours / 7 days OR 70 hours / 8 days |
| Restart | 34 consecutive hours off duty |
| Sleeper berth | Split: 7/3 or 8/2 |
| Short-haul exemption | 49 CFR 395.1(e) — 150 air-mile radius, return to work-reporting location within 14 hrs |
| Adverse driving conditions | +2 hours, 49 CFR 395.1(b) |
| Personal conveyance | Allowed under specific FMCSA guidance — not for moving loaded for the carrier's benefit |

## Driver Qualification File (49 CFR 391.51)

See [[driver-qualification-file]] for full checklist. Required contents in summary:
- Application (391.21)
- Previous-employer inquiries (391.23)
- MVR + annual MVR (391.23, 391.25)
- Road test cert or equivalent (391.31/391.33)
- Current medical examiner's certificate (391.41-45)
- Clearinghouse pre-employment + annual queries
- Drug & alcohol pre-employment test (Part 382)
- ELDT cert if applicable (Part 380 Subpart F)

## DVIR — Driver Vehicle Inspection Report (49 CFR 396.11, 396.13)

- Driver completes a DVIR at end of each driving day if any defect noted.
- Pre-trip inspection per 49 CFR 396.13 — driver reviews last DVIR before operating.
- Carriers must keep DVIRs noting defects ≥ 3 months along with repair certifications.

## Drug & Alcohol (49 CFR 382)

| Test type | When | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-employment | Before first dispatch | 382.301 |
| Random | Random selection from pool | 382.305 |
| Post-accident | Per 382.303 thresholds | 382.303 |
| Reasonable suspicion | Trained supervisor observation | 382.307 |
| Return-to-duty | After violation | 382.309 |
| Follow-up | Per SAP plan | 382.311 |

**Clearinghouse:** pre-employment full query + annual limited query for every CDL driver. Query records: 3 years. https://clearinghouse.fmcsa.dot.gov/

## Vehicle Maintenance (49 CFR 396)

- Systematic inspection, repair, maintenance program required.
- Annual periodic inspection — 49 CFR 396.17 — required by qualified inspector.
- Brake inspector qualifications — 49 CFR 396.25.
- Records: 1 year + 6 months after vehicle leaves carrier's control (varies — see Part 396).

## Cargo Securement (49 CFR 393 Subpart I)

- Working load limit ≥ 50% of cargo weight (aggregate of tiedowns).
- Minimum number of tiedowns by length and weight (393.110).
- Special commodity rules (393.112-126) — logs, dressed lumber, metal coils, paper rolls, concrete pipe, intermodal containers, automobiles, heavy machinery, flattened/crushed vehicles, large boulders, etc.

## Hazardous Materials

- 49 CFR 397 — driving and parking rules for hazmat
- 49 CFR 172 — communication (shipping papers, marking, labels, placards, emergency response info)
- 49 CFR 177 — carriage by public highway
- Hazmat endorsement (H or X) on CDL required for placardable loads
- Security plan + training every 3 years per 49 CFR 172.704

## CSA / FMCSA enforcement

- BASICs: Unsafe Driving, HOS Compliance, Driver Fitness, Controlled Substances/Alcohol, Vehicle Maintenance, Hazardous Materials Compliance, Crash Indicator.
- Inspection levels: I (full), II (walk-around), III (driver-only), IV (special study), V (vehicle-only), VI (radioactive), VII, VIII.
- DataQs for challenging inspection results: https://dataqs.fmcsa.dot.gov/

## Reportable incidents

- DOT-recordable accident — 49 CFR 390.5: fatality, injury treated away from scene, OR vehicle disabled requiring tow.
- OSHA recordable — 29 CFR 1904 — varies; days away, restricted, transferred (DART).
